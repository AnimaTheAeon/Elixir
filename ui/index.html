HOW IT DECIDES
  1. Finished files only. Torrents when Lifestream says they're done and in their final folder; other files once they've stopped growing for 5 minutes.
  2. Can't ask? Do nothing. If Lifestream, Midgar or Turks don't answer, automation pauses. Without Lifestream it only runs in folders marked "Nothing seeds from this folder".
  3. The owner does the work. Anything Midgar or Turks downloaded is theirs. A show Midgar tracks is handed to Midgar to import. Elixir names only what neither tracks.
  4. Sure, or it asks. A show or movie Elixir hasn't seen before waits here once. After that all four checks must pass: exact title, matching year, the episode exists, only one candidate.
  5. Nothing lost. Seeding files get a second name (hardlink or instant clone), never moved. Other files move within the same drive. Nothing is overwritten, copied or deleted.
  6. Undo for 30 days. Every step is written down first, so a restart can finish or roll back.
